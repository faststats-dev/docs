Session replays let you watch how people used your site: every page, click, scroll, and input, played back as it happened. Open **Replays** in a web analytics project's sidebar to see them.

Replays are recorded by the web SDK. See [Session Replay](/web-analytics/session-replay) to turn recording on and choose what is masked.

## Finding replays

The replay list shows each recorded session with the person or visitor, their browser, operating system, and country, when it ended, and how long it lasted. A dot marks replays nobody has watched yet. A replay is marked as watched as soon as someone opens it.

### Search

Type in the search field to find replays by session ID, person ID, email, name, browser, operating system, or country.

### Filters

Click **Filter** to narrow the list:

| Filter            | Matches                                                                         |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AI summary        | Replays that have, or don't have, an [AI summary](#summary)                     |
| AI pain points    | Summarized replays with, or without, pain points                                |
| Watched           | Replays someone has, or hasn't, watched                                         |
| Identified        | Sessions of [identified](/web-analytics/identify) people, or anonymous visitors |
| Errors            | Replays with, or without, an error                                              |
| Poor web vitals   | Replays with, or without, a poor [web vital](/web-analytics/web-vitals)         |
| Browser           | One or more browsers                                                            |
| Operating system  | One or more operating systems                                                   |
| Country           | One or more countries                                                           |
| Route visited     | Replays that visited a page, such as `/checkout`, or a pattern like `/docs/*`   |
| Poor vital        | Replays with a poor value for one metric: LCP, CLS, INP, FCP, or TTFB           |
| Click count       | Number of clicks, as a range or exact value                                     |
| Rage-click bursts | Number of rapid repeated clicks in one place                                    |
| Event count       | Number of recorded events                                                       |
| Duration (sec)    | Length of the replay in seconds                                                 |

Browsers, operating systems, and countries list the values of the loaded replays, and you can type in any other value. Picking several values matches replays with any of them.

#### Project fields

Below the built-in filters, the picker lists your project's fields and event properties. How they compare depends on their type:

| Type    | Operators                                                                     |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Text    | is, is not. Pick _&lt;field&gt; contains_ to match part of the value instead. |
| Number  | is, is not, greater than, less than                                           |
| Boolean | is                                                                            |

<Callout variant="info">
	You can also describe what you're looking for, for example _"rage clicks on
	Safari in Germany"_, and FastStats turns it into filters. If part of the
	request doesn't match a filter, you're told what was left out.
</Callout>

Next to the filters you can pick a [segment](/platform/funnels#saving-a-segment) to only show replays of people in it, or not in it. Click **Reset** to clear the filters and leave the open collection.

### Sorting

Sort by **Newest** (the default), **Oldest**, **Longest**, **Shortest**, **Most events**, **Fewest events**, or by ID.

## Collections

Collections group replays so you can come back to them. The collections pane lists them with the number of replays in each. On small screens, switch to the **Collections** tab. Click a collection to browse only its replays.

### Built-in collections

Every project starts with automatic collections you can't edit or delete:

| Collection                                        | Contains                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Watched, Not Watched                              | Replays someone has, or hasn't, watched                    |
| Error                                             | Replays with an error                                      |
| Poor Vitals                                       | Replays with a poor [web vital](/web-analytics/web-vitals) |
| Poor LCP, Poor CLS, Poor INP, Poor FCP, Poor TTFB | Replays with a poor value for that vital                   |

Replays opened from **Error** and the vitals collections start at the moment the error or poor vital happened.

### Creating a collection

Click **New collection**, give it a name, and choose a type:

- **Automatic:** replays that match its filters appear in it on their own, including new ones as they're recorded
- **Manual:** a watchlist you add replays to by hand

The type can't be changed after the collection is created.

#### Automatic collections

An automatic collection uses the same [filters](#filters) as the replay list, including [project fields](#project-fields). Replays that match all of them appear in it.

Under **Playback**, turn on **Start replay at matching moment** to open each replay at the moment its **Route visited**, **Errors**, or **Poor web vitals** filter matched, instead of at the beginning.

While browsing an automatic collection, its filters appear in the filter bar with a dashed border. Remove one to look at the collection more broadly without changing the saved collection.

#### Manual collections

Open a replay and click **Add to collection** in its header, then tick the collections it belongs in. You can also create a new manual collection from there, and the open replay is added to it.

### Editing and deleting

Open the **⋯** menu next to a collection to **Edit** or **Delete** it. Deleting a collection doesn't delete its replays.

## Watching a replay

Click a replay to open it. The header shows the page being played, and has buttons to:

- **Copy link to this moment:** a link that opens the replay at the current time
- **Add to collection** (see [Manual collections](#manual-collections))
- **Download as JSON:** save the recording. Drop an exported file onto the replay list later to watch it without uploading it. Opened files have no summary or comments.
- **Delete replay**

If someone used several tabs in one session, each tab is its own recording. Use the tab picker in the header to switch between them.

### Player controls

| Control            | What it does                                                                               |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Play / pause       | Starts or stops playback. Press `Space`.                                                   |
| Back / forward 10s | Jumps 10 seconds. Press `←` or `→`. Hold the button to rewind, or to play at double speed. |
| Previous / next    | Opens the previous or next replay in the list. Press `Shift` `P` or `Shift` `N`.           |
| Inspect            | Inspect the page's elements at the current moment. Press `I`.                              |
| Skip idle          | Skips stretches over 5 seconds where nothing happens                                       |
| Speed              | Plays at `0.5×`, `1×`, `2×`, `4×`, or `8×`                                                 |
| Fullscreen         | Press `F` or double-click the replay                                                       |

Hold down on the replay itself to play at double speed while you hold. The keyboard icon lists every shortcut.

Your speed and skip idle settings are remembered in your browser.

### Timeline

Drag along the timeline or click anywhere on it to jump. Hover it to see the time at that point. Markers show where something happened:

- **Red:** an error
- **Green:** a web vital
- **Primary color:** a [comment](#comments)

Click a marker to jump to it.

### Up next

When a replay ends, an end screen offers the previous and next replays from the list you opened it from. Turn on **Autoplay** to start the next one after 5 seconds. Close the end screen to see the last frame.

## Details

On the right of the player, tabs show what happened during the replay. Click any entry to jump to that moment.

### Summary

Click **Generate summary** to have AI watch the replay and write a short recap, with the pain points where the visitor ran into trouble. Each pain point is marked as a **Bug** (the product failed) or **Friction** (it worked as designed but still stopped the visitor). Click a pain point to jump to it.

AI summaries are still rolling out, so the tab may not be shown in every project yet. Replays need a full snapshot and at least two seconds of activity to be summarized. Summaries of recordings that are still in progress or incomplete only use what was recorded.

### Activity

Every page view, click, rage-click burst, input, and console message, in order. Search it, or filter it to one kind.

### Console

Console output from the session, filterable by **Errors**, **Warnings**, **Info**, and **Logs**, and searchable. Only shown when the replay has console output, which needs [console recording](/web-analytics/session-replay#console-recording).

### Network

Requests made during the session, filterable by **All**, **Fetch / XHR**, and **Errors**, and searchable by URL, method, or status. Only shown when the replay has requests, which needs [network recording](/web-analytics/session-replay#network-recording).

## Comments

Use the **Comments** tab to discuss a replay with your team.

- **Pinned to a moment:** a new comment is tied to the moment you're watching, shown as a chip in the comment box. Click the chip to post the comment without a time. Comments with a time appear as markers on the timeline, and clicking a comment's time jumps there.
- **Mentions:** type `@` to mention a teammate. They get a notification, and an email if they haven't read it within 15 minutes.
- **References:** type `#` to link a dashboard, chart, error, funnel, or data source
- **Replies:** click **Reply** on a comment to answer it. Everyone who has commented on the replay is notified of new comments.
- **Pin:** pin a comment to keep it at the top of the thread
- **Edit and delete:** you can edit your own comments. Click **edited** on a comment to see its earlier versions.

Press `Enter` to post and `Shift` `Enter` for a new line. Comments posted by an AI assistant through the FastStats MCP server are marked as such.

## Permissions

Viewing, editing, and deleting replays, collections, and comments follow your [organization role](/platform/organizations/roles). Buttons you don't have permission for are disabled and say why.
