The **Members** tab lists everyone in the organization with their role. Owners are listed first, then Admins, then everyone else. Use the search box to filter by name or email.

## Inviting people

Admins and Owners can invite people from the **Invitations** tab:

1. Click **Invite member**
2. Enter their email address
3. Pick the [role](/platform/organizations/roles) they'll have when they join
4. Click **Send Invite**

The invitee gets an email with a link to accept the invitation. If they already have a FastStats account, they also see it in their in-app notifications.

Invitations expire after 48 hours. The **Invitations** tab shows each pending invitation and when it expires. To withdraw one, open its menu and choose **Cancel invite**.

## Accepting an invitation

Opening the invitation link shows the organization, the role you've been offered, the email address it was sent to, and when it expires. Click **Accept invitation** to join, or **Decline**.

You need a FastStats account to accept:

- **If you have one**, sign in with the email address the invitation was sent to
- **If you don't**, choose **Create account**. The invitation lets you sign up even while sign-ups are invite-only

If the organization [requires two-factor authentication](/platform/organizations#requiring-two-factor-authentication), turn on 2FA for your account before accepting.

## Changing a member's role

Admins and Owners can change roles. On the **Members** tab, open the member's menu, choose **Change role**, and pick the new role.

An Owner's role can't be changed directly. [Transfer ownership](/platform/organizations#transferring-ownership) to someone else first.

## Removing a member

On the **Members** tab, open the member's menu and choose **Remove member**. They lose access to the organization and all of its projects right away.

- You can't remove yourself from this menu
- An Owner can't be removed. Transfer ownership first

Removing a member doesn't delete anything they created. Their dashboards, charts, and notebooks stay in the organization.
